About Wish Hounds Press

Wish Hounds Press is an independent imprint publishing horror and dark fiction: stories to haunt you.

Founded in 2024 by Tabitha Potts, the imprint takes its name from the legendary wish hounds of Dartmoor. Tabitha wishes to focus the imprint on publishing darker literary short fiction.

The legend of the wish hounds

In Devon folklore, the wish hounds are a pack of spectral black dogs said to hunt across Dartmoor by night, driven on by a dark huntsman. Wistman’s Wood, an ancient oak wood high on the moor, is said to be their kennel.

To hear them is an ill omen. The imprint takes its name from them.

About the author

Tabitha Potts is a writer and podcaster based in London. She is the author of the gothic novel The House of Dust and Shadows and runs Story Radio, a literary podcast.

Various of Potts’ short stories have been shortlisted in the Booker at Birkbeck Creative Writing Award judged by Ian McEwan, the MIR Folktale Festival, and the Royal Academy Pindrop Award, among others. She won Almasi League’s flash fiction competition judged by Courttia Newland. She received an Honourable Mention from the Alpine Fellowship Writing Prize in 2023 and was a Finalist in the London Independent Story Prize for Flash Fiction in 2026.

Potts is planning to publish a collection of her award-winning short and flash fiction, and an anthology of writing from the Story Radio podcast.
