Privacy

When you use the contact form, your name, email address and message are sent to Wish Hounds Press by a service called Web3Forms, and are used only to reply to you.

If you join the newsletter, your email address is stored by Mailchimp and used only to send the newsletter. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

Your details are never sold or shared. To ask for them to be deleted, use the contact form.
